Sroka for Android · Material 3
Android app
Built from Material 3 components: top app bars, search bar, list items, navigation bar and rail, FAB, chips, sheets, menus, dialogs, switches and progress indicators. They're themed with Sroka's dark palette (blue primary, yellow tertiary) and Outfit mapped onto the M3 type scale. Custom components appear only where M3 has nothing that fits.
