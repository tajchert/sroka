Find it now. Read it when you’re ready.

  1. 1

    Spot it

    Click the magpie in Chrome (Ctrl+Shift+S, ⌘⇧S on Mac), paste a link on the web, or share from any app on Android, iPhone, or iPad.

  2. 2

    Stash it

    Sroka grabs the clean article — text, images, comment threads, or PDFs — and tucks it into your synced library.

  3. 3 Works offline

    Read it

    Native mobile apps download saved articles automatically (over Wi-Fi by default, according to your settings). On a plane, on the train, or in a tunnel — it’s waiting.

Offline

No signal? Keep reading.

On Android, iPhone, and iPad, saved means offline — article text, images, and supported discussions download automatically according to your settings. Airplane mode, subway tunnels, mountain cabins: your list goes with you.

  • Downloaded on saveNo extra step. Saved articles download over Wi-Fi by default so they’re ready offline in your native apps.
  • Images & PDFs includedArticles and saved PDFs are stored locally so they open with or without a connection.
  • Progress syncs laterWhere you stopped in an article syncs across every device once you’re back online.

Organize & search

Find it again, even offline.

Tag your saves, star favorites, filter by quick reads, and archive what you’ve finished. On Android, iPhone, and iPad, full-text search looks inside your downloaded articles with zero signal.

Moving from Pocket or Instapaper?

Import your existing Pocket export, Instapaper export, or browser bookmarks right in Settings — and export your reading list as CSV or JSON whenever you like.

Calm reader

Just the words.

Ads, pop-ups, and clutter stay behind. Open any saved article in a calm reader with adjustable font, size, line spacing, and Light, Sepia, and Dark themes — or open saved PDFs right beside them.

Native craft

Built for phones, foldables, and tablets.

No sluggish webview wrappers. Android is built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with adaptive cover-screen and dual-pane foldable layouts; iPhone and iPad run native SwiftUI with split view and a native share sheet.

Open source · Apache 2.0

Built to stay around.

Pocket shut down in 2025, and a lot of us lost our reading shelf. Sroka is free, has no ads, and puts every line of its code in the open — hosted at sroka.app or self-hosted on your own Cloudflare account.

  • Free & open source

    Licensed under Apache 2.0. Web app, Chrome extension, Android, iOS, and Cloudflare Workers all live in one public repository.

  • Private by default

    Passwordless email code sign-in. Nobody tracks what you read, analytics stay off until you opt in, and you can delete your account and data anytime.

  • Self-host on Cloudflare

    Run the exact same serverless backend (Workers, SQLite Durable Objects, Queues, R2) on your own Cloudflare account and point the apps at your server.
