Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

  • Sroka saves articles so you can read them later. We keep what you save to show it back to you. We don't build profiles or show your saves to anyone.
  • No ads, no selling data, no AI training on your library.
  • Usage analytics are off unless you turn them on. Crash reports can be turned off in Settings.
  • Export your library or delete your account any time.

What we keep

  • Your email, to send you sign-in codes.
  • Your library: the links, pages, PDFs and imports you save, with tags, favorites and reading progress.
  • Sign-in sessions: IP address and device type, to keep your account secure.
  • Crash reports: device model, OS and app version, the error, and an app install ID. They never include what you save or read.
  • Push token (Android and iOS apps): a device address from Google or Apple, so we can quietly tell your phone that you saved something and it can download it for offline reading. These messages are empty signals: no links, titles or content. Signing out or deleting your account removes the token.
  • Usage analytics, only if you allow them: which features are used (for example "an article was saved"), with a random app ID. Never links, titles, searches or your email.
  • On your device: a sign-in cookie and your settings. The apps keep an offline copy of your library; signing out removes it.

We use this to run Sroka for you and keep it secure; analytics only with your consent.

How saving works

When you save a link, our server fetches the page and makes a clean, readable copy, so the website sees our server, not you. Pages saved with the browser extension and PDFs you upload stay private to your account.

To see which websites we should support better, we look at anonymous totals, for example which sites or public articles are saved most. A total only appears once at least 5 people have saved it, never shows who saved what, and never includes your private pages or PDFs.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage, sign-in emails and bot protection.
  • Google: Firebase Crashlytics (Android crash reports), Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android sync signals), Google Analytics (only if you allow it), and Google Fonts for our typeface.
  • Apple: Apple Push Notification service (iOS sync signals).

How long we keep it

Your account and library: until you delete them. Server logs: up to 30 days. Crash reports: up to 90 days. Analytics: up to 2 months.

Deleting your account

In the Android app or at sroka.app/app, open Settings → Delete account. Your account, library, files and sessions are deleted right away. Can't sign in? Email hello@sroka.app from your account's address and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at hello@sroka.app (export is also in Settings). If you're in the EU, you can also contact your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO).

Who we are

Sroka (sroka.app, the Android app and the browser extension) is run by Michal Tajchert, Poland. Sroka isn't meant for children under 16. If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app for anything important.
